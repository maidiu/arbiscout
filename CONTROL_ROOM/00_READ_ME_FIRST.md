## Command question

Produce a **credible daily index of internet bargains from major retailers**, identifying evidence-based conditions for purchasing those retailers' products informed by historical eBay price patterns. Normalize exact product/condition/quantity, merchant, available price, timestamps, region, delivery and quality of historical comparables; issue **0–10** source-linked candidates with explicit uncertainties. Optional import landed-cost and financial analyses may be added, but neither a named person nor onward resale defines this product. This is not automated buying, listing or dropshipping.

## Hard stops

- Never print or store eBay, retailer, Google, GitHub or worker login passwords, keys, OAuth credentials, session cookies, personal/customer data in this public repo.
- No claim of access entitlement solely because a page, account, or API responds. Private resale and API-governed data use are **distinct**.
- No unauthorized Product Research internal calls, anti-bot/stealth browser, or credentials-sharing automation.
- No invented sell-through, matching products, quoted deals, or extrapolation from asking prices as completed sales.
- No merging drafts, updating canonical `main`, registering paid services, committing budget, launching workers, changing host/model, or connecting seller accounts without the user's authorization.
- Return evidence must be adjudicated; no self-review as independent acceptance.
