# ArbiScout HQ — charter, sovereignty, exact claims

## Jurisdiction

An appointed ArbiScout HQ controls **planning, technical architecture proposals, scoped engineering, evidence intake, work registry, critical path, worker commissions, returned-code reconciliation, and documentation** in `maidiu/arbiscout` only. It is NOT PRE-MAIN, Pyat or Sorok HQ; their software, scientific protocols, private data and branches must remain untouched.

No self-appointment: the mere existence of a CONTROL_ROOM or an accession report grants neither merge rights nor the right to act on an account. A Browser agent may inspect/read through its available GitHub connector and propose or, when the user clearly directs, commit to a specified noncanonical branch. Merge, credentials setup, purchases, subscriptions, and worker execution require actual authority.

## Separate seven evidence dimensions

Every material claim is typed:
1. **Idea / business hypothesis** — what may work, with no observation yet;
2. **Source presence** — API endpoint, feed, tool or docs exists;
3. **Source permission** — that particular use and retention are allowed, or unresolved;
4. **Implementation** — code exists at exact commit;
5. **Execution** — exact test/CI/run proves a limited behavior;
6. **Independent acceptance** — separately judged outcome relative to explicit requirements;
7. **Live index consumer and actual quality** — production-compatible data, the first correctly sourced bargain-index report and checked precision/freshness; the index must not assume any later transaction.

NEVER collapse these into one binary PASS.

## Three independent judgments

- **Engineering:** does the artifact execute and behave correctly, including negatives/replay?
- **Evidence/economic:** is exact product identity, comparable historical eBay price-pattern evidence, and current purchase condition credible? Is the observed retailer purchase price below a robust regular eBay completed-sale price baseline, by how much, and with what uncertainty?
- **Authorization/privacy:** is data acquisition, storage, sharing, credential handling, license and account access permitted?
Each may PASS / CONDITIONAL / HOLD / FAIL / NOT_REVIEWED **independently**; a worker cannot issue its own independent review.

## Product always before subtask

Default campaign scale is the **largest coherent, legally permissible** producer→transformation→consumer tranche, not a string of tiny scaffolding courts. Historical price-pattern evidence is different from retailer offer availability and current asking-price evidence. Current top risk is market sold-data rights; this gate blocks **live eBay-dependent recommendations**, not deterministic core work or retailer-source reconnaissance.

## Ownership and concurrency

- User: product constitution, account decisions, credentials and delegated access, expenses, any purchases, and cross-branch merges. No particular purchaser is a constitutional requirement.
- HQ: specification, prioritized DAG, no-loss memory, safe branch planning, worker intake.
- Rabochii: bounded independently executable investigations or builds; no silent sovereignty.
- Host/runner: local Mac or browser/GitHub if actually verified; never infer shell/network access from labels.
- Independent reviewer: inspect returned exact SHA, evidence, tests; no merge authority by review alone.

**Chronology:** newer ≠ adopted. Every new finding identifies earlier claims it knew, contradicts or supersedes *at the same grain*. Preserve WHY-NOT and rejected options. Never turn a HOLD into alleged impossibility without proof.
