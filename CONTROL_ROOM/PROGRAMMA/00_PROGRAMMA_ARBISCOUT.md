# PROGRAMMA ARBISCOUT

**Status:** proposed whole-program constitution, not merged or live.

## Terminal product
An automated daily sourcing scout: find genuine domestic discounts from Walmart/Target/Best Buy and other authorized retail sources; optionally inspect AliExpress/Alibaba suppliers; match exact model, GTIN, variant, quantity and condition against genuinely comparable eBay completed-sales evidence; compute landed costs, shipping, marketplace fees and uncertainty; publish **up to ten**, never a mandatory ten, defensible opportunities for human purchase review. Later measure actual realized results and improve.

## R — Recognize, recover, reconcile
Recover existing architecture, draft PRs, alternative open-source code, vendor documentation, prior technical failures, bad results, reviews and precise real consumers. A discovered branch or sourcefile is not an integrated feature; a green fixture is not permission or live proof.

## O — Organize and adjudicate
The work registry is the whole-program DAG, cross-linked to the source registry, architecture, acceptance matrix, why-not log and dated current checkpoint. Work at nested levels: L0 finished business result; L1 domestic/import sourcing, eBay evidence, identity, finance, reporting, feedback, legal/permissions; L2 specific capabilities; L3 actual source/contract/code/test/consumer. Every item must show requirements, dependencies, status, owner, evidence and next action.

## I — Implement, prove, integrate, learn
Build the largest coherent lawful flow from source offer to evidence-backed report; test negatives and first actual consumer; independently review significant changes; adopt only through authorized merge. Feed new evidence and realized profit back into R/O. Prefer genuine product delivery to self-perpetuating documentation.

## Required separations
- Buying and owning discounted goods is not the same question as API data-use rights.
- An official API is not automatically licensed for resale analytics.
- eBay asking prices are not completed-sales evidence.
- Synthetic model outputs are not empirical results.
- Imports require MOQ, freight, duties, lead times and capital lock-up.
- Good local tests do not grant vendor access or establish true demand.
- No one is granted access, budget, worker deployment, production merge or buying authority by this document.
