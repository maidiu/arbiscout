## 2026-11-17 Dell capture and fixture validation

- **Capture:** one local read-only HTTP GET of `https://www.dell.com/en-us/shop/deals/dc`; Dell robots.txt allowed the path. Raw HTML stayed local: 2,137,332 bytes, SHA-256 `282354b2a263f37db709cc76a0f9251c42799df04887be5f0c63d722e783bf4c`.
- **Mechanism:** the Top Deals HTML contains `ProductsDetailInfo = JSON.parse("...")` with 36 embedded offer objects. No private API, sign-in, pagination or product-page crawl was used.
- **Extraction:** `dellPrice` is current; `marketPrice` is retained as a reference only when greater than current. 34 offers have both; 2 are current-only. `marketPrice` is not a paid or historical price.
- **Identity:** `productId`, `variantCode`, `pdUrl`, title and `snpStockStatus` are retained. GTIN and final configuration are not independently verified.
- **Implementation:** `ENGINEERING/arbiscout_app/providers.py` (`dell_products_from_html`, `fetch_dell_deals`) plus `--source dell` and `--snapshot`. Control room and catalog include Dell.
- **Tests:** synthetic `ProductsDetailInfo` fixture covers paired prices, null/weak market price, current-only rows, missing payload and catalog labeling. Local real capture produced 36 rows; local catalog test produced 36 Dell rows.
- **Status:** FIXTURE_VALIDATED, not LIVE_VALIDATED. Dell terms/permission, retention, configuration/GTIN accuracy and independent review remain open.
