## Other candidate universe (documentary HOLD, not hidden proof)
- https://www.worthpoint.com/ : collectibles/antiques historical reference, broad programmatic eBay row-feed and suitable license **not verified**.
- https://130point.com/sales/ : specialist human lookups, no qualified broad API/retention rights evidenced.
- https://www.zikanalytics.com/ and https://www.shelftrend.com/ : research dashboards, no independently verified general authorized per-sale transaction endpoint for ArbiScout.
- Price archive resellers, open GitHub wrappers and public scrape caches: no identified lawful, continuously refreshed, exact-item actual-paid dataset. A repository MIT license applies to its code, not to eBay's content.
- Direct eBay business partner/data-license inquiry: potentially highest-quality durable route but **no present purchasable grant, schema, pricing or availability** documented. This is a request, not a source acquisition.

## Evidence hierarchy / required contradictions audit
1. Official eBay text can establish eBay's own claimed product capabilities and restrictions, **not** user entitlement or verified output.
2. Vendor docs can establish *offer*, endpoint schema, plan and vendor obligations, **not** actual sale truth, comprehensive coverage or eBay upstream authorization.
3. A supported response after an authorized account signup can establish *functionality*, but price accuracy requires official audit; eBay permission remains separate.
4. A vendor's sponsored/SEO "comparison" of competitors cannot serve as an independent benchmark.
5. 'Sold', 'completed', 'removed', 'best offer accepted', 'displayed ask', 'last price', 'paid amount' and 'per-listing average' are different observations. No innocent coercion between them.
6. For the target population, disclose missing accepted offers, empty or truncated pages, title collision, region, condition, year, currency, per-unit quantity and shipping. Vendor-only result counts cannot be treated as total eBay sales.
7. Document exact provider response version, terms revision and marketplace at any later live test. **No later evidence has been acquired in this return**.
