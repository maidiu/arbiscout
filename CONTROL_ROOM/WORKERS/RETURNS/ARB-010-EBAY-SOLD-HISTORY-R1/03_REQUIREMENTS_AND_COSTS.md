# ARB-010 R1 — Access, permissions, technical requirements and cost model
**As observed:** October 31, 2026. Pricing below is **advertised supplier list pricing**, not a negotiated quote, actual paid invoice or permission approval. Revalidate before signup. USD where suppliers state USD. No new accounts, trials, credentials, API subscriptions, paid access or automated eBay requests were used.

## Non-negotiable supplied-data shape
A qualifying row must distinguish **the exact completed-sale transaction** from active inventory, completed unsold listing, a listing-level multi-unit average, a displayed ending ask and an offer upper bound. Require transaction or stable sale record ID, sold timestamp with zone, realized paid item amount where actually substantiated, currency, shipping charged/known/unknown, product GTIN/UPC/EAN and brand+model/variant, item condition, pack quantity, listing category, marketplace/site and market locality, seller-input provenance where allowed, source URL/ID, row first/last seen, terms/version and query. Preserve original and normalized amounts separately.

We need enough row-level observations, sample coverage and censoring rates to estimate regular historical same-product price. Distinguish observed raw count from declared full-population count, per-page count, missing offer count, unknown shipping, max-page exhaustion, older-archive selection, event quantity and time range. Calendar 90d vs 1y vs 3y is not adequate alone: require actual sold-window lower/upper dates and evidence of omitted rows. For multiunit listing `quantitySold=5`, price per sale/lot is not necessarily one unit; reject for baseline until unit matching and price basis are explicit.

## Reproducible budget equation
Let **N = product identities checked daily**, **D = 30 days**, **q = distinct exact-product query variants/regions per item**, **p = charged pages per query**, **h = additional hydration/offer lookups**, **r = fraction of retried requests that get charged**. Approximate **monthly billable units = N × D × q × p × (1+r) + h**. The optimistic unit-floor below fixes **q=1, p=1, h=0, r=0**, no historical backfill and a fresh check every day. It does **not** demonstrate one page suffices or each vendor charges per request rather than credit differently. Page/row limits and approved caching will change real bills; unapproved indefinite caching is excluded.

**Multi-page sensitivity:** 3 pages × two query variants = **6×** base. Then 100/day → 18k/mo; 1k/day → 180k/mo; 10k/day → 1.8m/mo *before hydration*. For monthly cache refresh every seven days (if expressly permitted and scientifically tolerable), approximate 30/7 vs 30 runs per identity, but eBay sale recency and current retailer changes must be considered separately. Changing cache policy does not create authorization or broaden observed sale history.

**Worked example, SoldComps:** 1,000 products/day × 30 × 2 distinct variants × 3 paid pages = **180,000 base units**; if `hydrateBoa` consumes one additional charge on half of queried pages, add **90,000 units** = **270,000 monthly**. The $549/500k advertised tier might cover this budget *if* charge semantics are as assumed, but **there is no validated coverage, paid-price accuracy, usable upstream grant or successful call**. Verify the hydration billing statement and rate-limit before reliance.

## Query/selectivity, retention and technical operations questions
- Exact ID lookup vs title-text matching: require GTIN, MPN, ePID, model+revision, color/size/region/voltage and exact unit count; score candidate matches separately and never promote weak keyword hit.
- Backend status codes, cursor consistency, query term semantics, duplicate item IDs across pages, maximum page window/total-count guarantees, marketplace switching and currency handling are all **unverified live**.
- Separate query quota from credits, billing events, 429 behavior, request burst/per-minute, latency, 90th/99th percentile and paid offer lookups; get written terms before comparing sustained scale.
- Storage right, expiry date, removal obligations, personal seller data, cache lifetime, redistribution, derived public statistics and usage for **third-party retailer purchase research** must be answered provider by provider. **No accounts or subscription purchases authorized by this return.**
- Downstream first consumer: single JSON sale observation of exact product with typed actual-price claim, provenance, window and uncertainties → `ARB-040`/ `ARB-051`, not any downstream selling economics.
