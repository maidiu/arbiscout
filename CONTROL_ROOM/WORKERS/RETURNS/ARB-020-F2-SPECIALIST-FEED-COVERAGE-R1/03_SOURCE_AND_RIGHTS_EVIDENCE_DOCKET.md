## Chronology and contradiction court
- **Acme 70k vs 91k**: original old dev affiliate text says 70k+, which cannot be applied as a current measured value. Any different figure in prior work likewise requires current feed audit; we do **not** average claims. Live deals confirm current sale surfaces, not underlying catalog count.
- **Northern Tool**: contemporaneous first-party page appears current when crawled, but may be orphaned static content; third-party Apr 19 removal precise, not independently official CJ confirmation. Status CONFLICTING / HOLD; no fake inactive certainty.
- **SCHEELS**: creator “Simple Product Feed” supports manual storefront linking; no declared file schema or bulk export. Status NO_BULK_PROOF, not confirmed feed.
- **REI Outlet**: “Affiliate program includes Outlet” ≠ “Outlet rows supplied in daily feed”. Mark outlet-inclusion NULL.
- **Woot**: public official endpoint truly enumerates distinct deal categories but actual account key, permission to retain/commercially index and full row content remain untested. Public endpoint existence is not legal entitlement.
- **Awin one-time deposit**: do not transpose advertiser package monthly pricing to publisher; $5 refundable security amount in older publisher docs could still create upfront friction and was not paid.
- **Petco**: current Impact listing vs long-form legacy CJ agreement require issuer and effective-date clarification.
- **Tire Rack**: express no-affiliate-price-display clause is a concrete negative counterexample even if technically extensive inventory available.
- **Academy**: noncommissionable hot deal does not *logically* prove omitted feed, so reject automatic clearance-feed interpretation in both directions.
- **Meh family**: one first-party shared affiliate program does not establish one API schema across three branded retail storefronts.
- **Conditional coupons**: Acme login-only stacked promotions, REI member price and Fanatics apparel variants cannot be normalized as universally available simply by reading list/sale price.

## Rights verification template (unsent)
Require a written program/contract answer identifying ArbiScout's real role: automated purchase-research **comparing current your-store purchase prices with independently licensed actual completed eBay historical sale prices**; no future resale operations. Ask: (1) approved discovery/download and scope, (2) whether store/clearance feed is complete and available daily with identifiers, (3) storage/TTL, user-facing price snippets, links/attribution and historical-derived gap numbers, (4) region/checkout validation restrictions and automated frequency, (5) whether current retailer/advertiser agreement requires traffic, website, sales or removal upon termination, (6) whether any unavoidable recurring platform or data-provider charges exist, (7) delegated access/credentials approved on local Mac; never ask for passwords/cookies.

## Hard adverse evidence rules
Document-only claims remain DOCUMENTARY_ONLY and do not become LIVE_TESTED from an affiliate signup page. Product category breadth is not eBay SKU sale volume. A raw 40% MSRP markdown is not proof the retailer price is below eBay completed price. If a source cannot provide licensed identifiers, actual buyable price and a trustworthy current stock check, store its record as REJECT/UNKNOWN, not a 0–10 bargain. No third-party agreement confers eBay historical sold-data rights.
