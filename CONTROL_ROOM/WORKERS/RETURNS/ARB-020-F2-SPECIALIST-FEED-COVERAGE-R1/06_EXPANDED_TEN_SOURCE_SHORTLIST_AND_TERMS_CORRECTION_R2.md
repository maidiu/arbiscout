## CRITICAL R2 CONTRADICTION: original top-three Adorama must be demoted (P0)

Initial F2 listed Adorama as #2 because official https://www.adorama.com/g/adorama-affiliate-program says *robust product feed* and https://www.adorama.com/api/getContent?contentName=affiliate_faq&output=desktop says it is free to join and Impact-approved partners get product feed. **Independent deeper first-party terms check** https://www.adorama.com/api/getContent?contentName=Affiliate_Agreement&output=desktop shows **Section 7 prohibiting affiliates from including price information in product descriptions or otherwise on the affiliate site**. **Section 8(2) prohibits caching licensed materials**, and Section 8(1) constrains rights/use to affiliate duties or internal research. The same agreement names **Partnerize** as Manager whereas the FAQ says **Impact**. This may reflect legal-page vintage/contract mismatch; it cannot be “averaged away” or assumed superseded. Even if access is granted and free, these terms on their face conflict with a user-visible **price-gap index** and persistent local feed store. **Disposition: NOT ELIGIBLE UNDER UNEXCEPTED PUBLISHED AGREEMENT; only reconsider on current binding terms or specific written grant explicitly allowing price-display, use and cache/derived retention.** Thus Adorama is no longer a top-three first attempted source. A feed is not usable for this product merely because it exists.
