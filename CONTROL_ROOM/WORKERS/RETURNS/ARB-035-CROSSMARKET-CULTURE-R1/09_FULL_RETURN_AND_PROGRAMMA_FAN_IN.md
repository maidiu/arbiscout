## Most important substantive findings / adverse evidence
1. Sector-level eBay importance (eBay FY2025 10-K) and seller survey preferences cannot prove exact-item sales volume, unit value, markdown recurrence or comparative gaps.
2. Home Depot **daily events**, LEGO sale pages, Adorama special bundles and Harbor Freight dated coupons evidence **promotional mechanisms**, not repeat markdown events in the same SKU and not a normal historic paid eBay price.
3. Specialist buyers may **out-monitor** general bargain shoppers. LEGO and TCG forums already discuss store coupons, inventory, item numbers and rapid Discord/deal alerts; counterevidence is selected, but enough to refute any categorical 'niche buyers uninformed' hypothesis.
4. Cross-channel separation can arise from **ZIP-specific inventory, repair urgency and exact-part search**, not buyer ignorance; a geographical gap that cannot be purchased by a target buyer fails application acceptance.
5. **Historical source truth is critical:** official actual Best Offer considerations vs third-party asking-price upper-bound substitutions; title-based/kit/condition mismatch can create fictitious gaps.
6. A high ticket with a single sale in 90 days is NOT recurring demand; transaction sample regularity and conservative eBay lower quartile matter.
7. Economic search cost/price dispersion literature supports investigation of competing mechanisms, NOT a direct estimate of 2026 eBay and retailer audience overlap.
8. The **strongest experimentally useful research priorities**, not measured winners, are OEM replacement auto parts, branded power tools/batteries, camera optics, business networking/components, sealed LEGO; controls mainstream gaming electronics, intensely monitored collectibles, unmatchable white-label bulky goods. No empirical top-ten rank is justified.
