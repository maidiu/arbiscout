# ARB-035-R / 02 — Causal rival court: ten mechanisms and observational equivalence
Scope: ARB-035 file 00 H1–H10, 03–05 and 08; frozen target SHA af342308e26b886a838dbd474cb1442a0ad943ae. These are prospective mechanisms without direct experimental measurement.

## Fundamental identification failure
A present retailer checkoutable quote R(t) below a trailing 90-day eBay paid-price median B90(t) is a **comparison of different markets and different time supports**, not itself evidence that eBay buyers missed the promotion. B90 includes up to 90 days of earlier paid observations by design. After a sudden retailer drop, the historical median cannot instantly update even if every eBay buyer learns of it at t. A historical gap may persist mechanically under full information. Actual contemporaneous purchases may remain at higher eBay prices due to urgency, shipping speed, payment/service trust, eBay gift credits, local access, warranty, manufacturer serial/licensing and buyer-specific friction.

Correct three questions separately: (i) Is the source-linked retailer purchase price genuinely lower than a robust historical reference? (product research); (ii) does the gap persist in contemporaneous *eligible offers* and subsequent price-paid events? (market adjustment); (iii) did channel-attention separation *cause* persistence? (causal sociology). Evidence for (i) does not imply (ii) or (iii). Do not import a causal claim into ARB-071 index output.

## Causal inference designs: graduated, not magical
1. **Product-suitability audit** first: independently verify exact identity and obtainable R(t), qualified actual paid-sales reference and shipping/tax/condition basis. This produces the ArbiScout index's descriptive evidence without attention theory.
2. **Event observational panel** next: time-stamp permitted merchant price drops, stock, location, duration and contemporaneous eBay recent actual-paid sale events, with pre-declared comparable unaffected exact-SKU controls and matched model generation. Observe eBay price response in event time, but treat no immediate 90-day median change as tautological.
3. **Attention diagnosis** separately: recruit consented **actual category buyers** through defensible frames not only deal forums, predefine retailer-monitoring and alert exposure before purchase, record awareness, geographic purchase eligibility, shipping urgency and non-price service. No buyers' private eBay accounts or location histories without separate explicit grant, no public repo PII.
4. **Stronger effect attribution** if feasible: compare an exogenous retailer promotion rollout/stagger across stores or regions with matched non-promoted SKUs, pre-trend checks, overlapping stock and shipping, promotions not chosen for impending demand collapse. A public post is nonrandom; causal estimates remain conditional.
5. Report descriptive opportunity detection separately from buyer-ignorance causation and adjust for SKU price transition, stock depletion, treatment selection and source selection.

## Historical-material caveat
Retailer inventory liquidation, controlled manufacturer distribution, platform category design and buyer urgency are institutional mechanisms; no individual 'ignorance' is necessary. If the measured category produces real all-in purchase gaps, the index can display them without claiming a channel-culture cause. Where geography or entitlement prevents acquisition, no purchasable index opportunity exists for that target locale, regardless of the alleged asymmetry.
