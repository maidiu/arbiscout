# ARB-035-R / 03 — Historical price, yield inference, uncertainty and Pareto review
Scope: files 03/06/07 at exact ARB-035 frozen SHA. No empirical sale data was supplied; all equations below are methodological evaluations, not observed product prices.

## Historical-price reference and the purchase-side comparator
For a legal, enumerated, time-stamped sample of equivalent completed sales i, define observed true consideration P_i with price type ACTUAL_PAID and item/shipping components documented. Condition, model revision, quantity, bundle and geography must match. B50 = empirical median(P_i), B25 = 25th percentile(P_i) and R = obtainable total retailer acquisition price at that locale/time. Proposed G50=B50-R and G25=B25-R are **valid descriptive arithmetic only if price bases are comparable**. Do not call an asking headline under Best Offer an actual P_i; record UNKNOWN_ACTUAL_PAID and refuse. Do not combine single-unit and multiunit lots, new/refurb, camera bundles and bare bodies or counterfeit/genuine parts.

A new-in-box retailer checkout R including tax but eBay item-only P_i excludes tax/shipping is a non-like-for-like comparison; alternatively comparing R all-in to actual eBay total item+shipping excluding unknown tax is conservative in one direction but is **not** an identical total-cost measure. Declare one consistently applied reference basis, carry eBay tax unobserved as missing, offer a second sensitivity lane, and abstain if differences can reverse gap sign. Avoid imputed tax as factual paid price. Date/time market FX and delivery geography matter. eBay paid shipping fields can reflect listing quote rather than amount paid; type separately.

## Provisional n90 >= 12 and weeks >= 6 are not sufficient for inferential certification
An empirical quartile with n=12 hinges on about the third or fourth order statistic. Resampling 12 transactions can produce wildly varying lower-tail estimates, particularly if a few exceptional offers are included. '6 active weeks' permits heavy concentration of sales in one promotion/seller cluster, giving effective independent n much smaller than 12. An ordinary iid percentile bootstrap has poor finite-sample tail coverage and is not made accurate by simply using a block bootstrap over six weeks. A 95% lower CI for B25-R can be computed mechanically but its coverage is not demonstrated. Define a deterministic data-insufficient refusal if few independent time/seller/listing clusters, unobserved missing Best Offer rates, unstable price trend, bundled ambiguity or excessive interval width. No arbitrary universally valid numeric cutoff may substitute for design-based coverage simulation/calibration.

**Better method:** Quantile sensitivity across accepted-offer-complete subsets, 30/90/365 time bands, effective cluster counts and local-price drift. Use nonparametric order-statistic/binomial quantile confidence bounds when the iid assumption is plausible and adequately sized; use block/cluster bootstrap when there are sufficient real independent blocks. Explicitly publish quantile rule, confidence-tail level, missingness bounds, and fallback 'INSUFFICIENT_DATA'. Resampling weakly identified values cannot rescue missing transaction-level paid consideration.

## Regular historical price versus temporal change
A 90-day rolling median (or p25) of past sales is a *lagging* reference by definition. Identify sale events' original timestamps, product revision, promo/regime breaks, price level changes and last-sale age. Use reference computed only from sales strictly **before** a deal timestamp in pilot predictive/temporal evaluation; do not let future data leak into earlier reference. Report 30d recent, 90d and 365d context; drop or segment a baseline when model retirement or competitor promotions structurally change the regime.

Demand regularity is n completed actual unit sales *conditional on available provider coverage*. It is not eBay buyer count, independent demand events or sell-through probability without measured exposure/supply. Count separate active weeks, long gaps, seller/listing concentration and cancellations/returns where permitted. Duplicate provider records and listing-quantity sold can distort denominators. Incomplete archival coverage is NOT zero sales.

## Yield denominator and intervals
The proposed yield formula 1000 * qualifying observed offers / offers scanned is well-defined only for a prespecified, consistently enumerated source/time/locale frame. The 07 mixed 40 curated deals+20 catalog+20 reverse-eBay+20 forced-discount control does **not** have a single retailer-offer sampling inclusion probability. Pooled n=100 binomial/Wilson inference is invalid: the strata intentionally oversample high eBay demand and high markdown outcomes. Time-observation repeats or multiple feed hits for same merchant offer must not create extra independent units.

For a genuine probability sample of each source frame h, estimate p_h = sum_i(w_hi*y_hi)/sum_i(w_hi), w_hi=1/inclusion probability (with correct finite-frame bookkeeping); Y_h=1000 p_h. Report frame-qualified estimates (curated feed, full catalog, outlet, local clearance separately). A cross-source union estimate requires a known, deduplicated cross-source frame and correct multiple-inclusion probability; do not add estimates with arbitrary 4:2:2:2 weights. Cluster uncertainty at least by SKU/merchant/time event and stratify source, sector and locale. Report nonqualified items and missing/non-queried ones explicitly; don't exclude after seeing failures. Wilson interval only for approximately iid Bernoulli draws from the *same* frame, not mixed convenience samples or repeated daily records.

Power: with zero in ten independent trials, a one-sided 95% binomial upper bound is 1-(.05)^(1/10) ≈0.259; 10 observations cannot rank sectors with low opportunity rates. Even ten bona fide randomly selected cases give wide uncertainty; preregister power and material difference before a sector-winner decision. Distinguish diagnostic controls from population estimation, and exploration from temporally independent replication.

## Pareto theory
Separate objectives G25 lower bound (absolute dollars), valid unique offer yield per 1000, historical sample regularity, same-SKU markdown recurrence, price/stock persistence, and evidence/permissions completeness. These variables are often different units and frames. 'A dominates B iff no worse on every axis' is a mathematical definition, not a measurement method or evidence that there are nondominated sectors. Uncertain estimated frontiers are especially fragile: bootstrap/hierarchical simultaneous uncertainty intervals over multiple objectives and sectors; announce unrankable overlap; only compare like-for-like standardized source/locale/time populations. Do not multiply a guessed attention score by gap dollars; attention is a separately tested cause, not a reward parameter.

## Direct positive/negative acceptance table
- Actual paid price/type unavailable or Best Offer ask-only: REFUSE PAID BASELINE.
- Same part number but materially different fitment/revision, bundle, firmware licence, battery inclusion or warranty grade: REFUSE MATCH.
- Retailer price promotion present but no checkout-valid stock, required coupon unavailable, only remote ZIP pickup or mandatory freight/tax unknown: REFUSE OR LABEL LOCALE-CONDITIONAL WITHOUT GENERALIZING.
- Sparse actual paid sales, nonstationarity, tail CI unstable, severe correlated-seller concentration: REFUSE INDEX QUALIFICATION; preserve study row.
- Source API responds but publisher rights or retention/display missing: HOLD DATA USE.
- Valid strict match, independently proved current R, actual paid eBay matched B25 and adequately calibrated conservative uncertainty lower bound > R, sufficiently material predeclared gap and robust regular history: *candidate index qualified for that locale*, not future sale or profit prediction.
