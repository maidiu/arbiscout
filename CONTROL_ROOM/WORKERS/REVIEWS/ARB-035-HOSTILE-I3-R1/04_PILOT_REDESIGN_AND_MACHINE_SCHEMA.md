## Revised pilot: independent probability sample and distinct diagnostic arms
**Primary estimand A:** per 1,000 unique *merchant-offer × checkout locale × scan time* units enumerated by an authorized predefined source frame (e.g. ShopSavvy curated feed for retailer/category/date; another estimand for a full Best Buy product catalog). Qualification indicator y=1 only after exact identity, true purchase, actual-paid eBay history and admissible conservative gap pass. For absent required data: label outcome *UNRESOLVED*, not zero qualified by fiat; separately report verified qualification rate lower bound (unknown counted nonqualified for that operational-index metric), an uncertainty/ascertainment rate and a conservative upper sensitivity bound. Never silently discard missing history after sampling.

**Primary estimand B:** probability an offer can be *fully evaluated* (source/rights and exact data completeness), important for achievable report yield. **C:** distribution of fully evidenced G25 and sample regularity conditional on qualified observation (not a population whole-category gap mean unless missingness controlled). **D:** repeat SKU markdown events per SKU-time exposure with censoring and source window. **E:** permitted, separately recruited buyer-attention measurement, never estimated from Reddit comments.

### Core probability population (separate from controls)
- Predeclare sector, *source frame*, locale and collection dates. Enumerate each eligible merchant-feed/catalog page under actual authorization with stable offer IDs and known number N_h and pagination coverage. If partial truncation, label FRAME_INCOMPLETE and restrict estimator to explicit accessible frame.
- Random selection within each h without knowing eBay prices or discount outcomes, using reproducible seed and inclusion probabilities pi_i. Sampling may deliberately oversample sectors, but design-weighted sector estimates must honor their denominators. At least two independent merchants per chosen sector if availability/rights permit; report absent frame rather than substitute lucky examples.
- For a minimal feasibility start, 10 sectors × 10 **random prospective retail offers each**, *all from enumerated legitimate frames*, is descriptive with large intervals; a powered winner comparison requires larger samples. Better allocate 100 across a smaller set of feasible merchant×sector frames to establish producer viability, then second-stage expand.
- Count each unit once per declared time snapshot; cross-posted SKU/repeated feed entries dedup with overlap provenance. Report unique offers, attempted eBay lookups, matched, true-paid data available, stock validated, eligible positive gap, and all refusal reason counts. A deal feed is selected by publisher: its estimate is expressly 'qualifying among feed-returned deals', not among all retailer offers.

### Separate diagnostic arms (excluded from yield denominator)
- **Reverse eBay chosen:** deliberately select recent high-sold-number model/MPNs; ask whether non-sale merchant listings exist and characterize negative gap. Appropriate case-finding, not denominator data.
- **Deep-discount negative/positive stress:** selected high MSRP percentage discounts; reveal false bargain behavior, identity failure, eBay sparsity. Not randomly offered probability sample.
- **Fast-alert collectible controls:** investigate sale propagation, missing stock and forum alert timing; nonrepresentative.
- **Sparse historical/Best Offer confusion:** injected synthetic negative tests and real permitted manual checks for invalid paid-price sources.
These arms may preserve the 2+2 per panel original concept **only** if published outside the 100 denominator and labeled controls; if resources cap 100 total, allocate fewer core cases and report even greater uncertainty. Never mix purposeful controls with estimated frame yield.

## Time and uncertainty
Prospective pre-promo retailer ordinary prices, dated source stock and true coupon/checkout; repeated daily authorized merchant observations for 12 weeks with permitted t0+6h,+24h,+72h,+7d snapshots when feasible. This can measure observed events in 12 weeks, **not annual seasonal recurrence**. A 365-day claim needs a full year prospective series or independently licensed dated historical SKU-level merchant events, with observation gaps/selection documented. Seller/eBay 7/30/90/365 paid-sale windows only if source permits actual timestamped relevant data. Split training/exploration and later independent out-of-sample month/retailer/time period; freeze analysis before holdout. Cluster/stratified sampling uncertainty and multiple-comparison correction before sector leader declaration; n=10 cannot rank low rates.

**Illustrative acceptance logic:** sample drawn → GTIN/MPN/contents exact → merchant of record/condition/warranty/locale/purchaser price verified → rights pass → actual paid eBay sale events complete enough → pre-period robust baseline and sensitivity → conservative G25 interval clearly >0 plus predeclared material dollar gap → index candidate. Record a refusal at each failed stage; no promotion badge accepted as gap.
