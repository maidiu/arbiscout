## Source-law and external route defects cross-cutting
**P0 — full producer gate.** ARB-010 PR #4 is an **unadjudicated** historical sold-data vendor survey, not proof of official machine-access permission or actual Best Offer paid-price fidelity. ARB-020 isolated return is **unadjudicated** retail discovery investigation, not a granted licensed consumer. Each data source needs its own permit, actual access, acceptable retention/publication policy, response completeness and representative exact-SKU test. Failure in any one layer blocks a qualified daily index entry; it need not block source-free code or documentary research.

**P1 — acquisition price and market price comparability.** Actual merchant seller-of-record, entitlement, coupon, stock, US ZIP shipping, tax, freight and condition need verified purchase-side evidence; actual paid eBay price, item + paid shipping, jurisdiction, bundle and timestamp need transparent reference-side evidence. If actual paid price is an upper bound of accepted Best Offer ask, refuse rather than assume. Where tax treatment cannot be aligned, show explicit sensitivity and refuse signs vulnerable to that ambiguity.

**P1 — mistaken causal “persistence.”** A static 90-day trailing median will mechanically lag a sudden markdown under full market knowledge. Do not use price persistence in historical aggregate as evidence of buyers' delayed attention. Analyze new sale events and accessible stock with matched controls independently of the index's descriptive purpose.

**P1 — research ranking calibration.** A Pareto frontier based on incomplete vendor-selected feeds and n=10 convenience cases is not a sector ranking. Predeclare frame and weights, holdout markets/time, uncertainty over multiple sector comparisons and distinct SKU independence. At least one genuine product gap must be demonstrably source-rights compliant before any end-to-end winner claim.
